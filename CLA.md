## 7. Acceptance and recordkeeping

A Contributor must explicitly affirm this Agreement through a maintainer-approved and documented process associated with the Contribution. The maintainers must verify the Contributor's authority and record that consent before merging third-party code.

The repository's PR template contains an acknowledgement prompt. **A checkbox alone does not automatically create an independently verified legal signature**, and the repository currently has no automated CLA-signing service. A CLA bot or other verified signing process may be introduced later.

Questions and any special contribution or employer agreements should be resolved before submitting code.
